ℹ️ About CalAge

CalAge is a free online toolkit created by Alfaiz Ansari. It brings together age calculation, birthday tools, date utilities, a simple calculator and a fun quiz/puzzle zone.

📖 How It Works

  • 🎂 Age Calculator compares your date of birth with the current date.
  • 📅 Date Difference calculates the approximate time between two selected dates.
  • 🎉 Birthday tools calculate the next occurrence of your birthday.
  • 🧠 Games run directly in your browser and do not require an account.
Important: CalAge is intended for general informational and calculation purposes. For important official calculations, verify results independently.
